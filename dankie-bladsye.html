What to do with them

Print Die Maand first and stick it inside a kitchen cupboard door, blank. When the school list lands, you'll have somewhere to put it.

Then fill in one week by hand on Die Week — and give every single line a name in the wie vat dit? column, even when the name is yours. Count how many say your name. That number is the conversation to have at home tonight.

Print as many as you need, for as long as you need them. Write in pencil.

Free to print and use for your own family. Please don't sell it or pass it off as your own — otherwise, share it as widely as you like. Send it to the mamma in your class group who is always the one who knows.
